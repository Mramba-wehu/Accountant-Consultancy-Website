Our Core Service Areas

Providing clarity and building trust through professional excellence.

+
External Audit & Assurance Services
We provide independent and objective assessments of your financial statements to ensure compliance with statutory requirements and international standards. Our audit approach focuses on risk identification and internal control evaluation, providing stakeholders with the assurance they need.
+
Internal Audit and Risk Advisory
Our internal audit services go beyond compliance. We help you identify operational inefficiencies, mitigate risks, and strengthen your internal control framework to safeguard assets and enhance organizational performance.
+
Tax Compliance, Planning & KRA Advisory Services
We help you navigate the complex tax landscape in Kenya. Our services include tax planning to optimize your liabilities, preparation and filing of returns (PAYE, VAT, Income Tax), and representing you during KRA audits and disputes.
+
Accounting, Bookkeeping & Financial Reporting
Maintain accurate financial records with our professional bookkeeping services. We prepare comprehensive financial reports (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheets, Cash Flow) that provide a clear picture of your business's financial health.
+
IFRS-Compliant Financial Statements Preparation
We ensure your financial statements are prepared in strict accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS). This ensures global comparability and transparency for investors, lenders, and regulators.
+
Payroll Management Services
Outsource your payroll to experts. We handle calculations, tax deductions (PAYE, NHIF, NSSF), and disbursement, ensuring timely and accurate payments while maintaining full compliance with Kenyan labor laws.
+
Budgeting, Cost Control & Financial Analysis
Plan for the future with confidence. We assist in creating realistic budgets, implementing cost control measures, and performing deep financial analysis to identify trends and growth opportunities.
+
Business Advisory & Strategic Consultancy
Partner with us for strategic growth. We provide expert advice on business structuring, investment opportunities, and long-term strategic planning to help your business achieve its full potential.
